    About me, Advocate Augustin N Khristi

    Advocate Augustin N Khristi holds a B.Com. and an LL.B. and was enrolled as an advocate in 2026. His practice covers customs, foreign trade policy, company law and intellectual property.

    His work sits where trade, customs and business law meet. He assists importers, exporters and manufacturers with MOOWR, SCOMET classification, EPCG, and BIS and QCO compliance, so that they understand the rules that apply to their goods and meet them on time.

    On the company side, he handles the incorporation of private and public limited companies, one person companies, LLPs, partnership firms and Section 8 companies, along with business registrations and annual ROC compliance. On the intellectual property side, he works on trademarks, copyright, patents, designs and geographical indications.

    He follows notifications from BIS, CBIC, DGFT, the Income Tax Department, MCA, RBI and SEBI, and explains them in plain language on this website.

    He also takes general legal matters, from drafting agreements and sending legal notices to civil and consumer cases.
