    About me, Advocate Augustin N Khristi

    Advocate Augustin N Khristi is an advocate who combines training in commerce and law. He holds a B.Com. and an LL.B., enrolled as an advocate in 2026, and practises in customs, foreign trade policy and business law.

    His work sits where trade, customs and company law meet. He helps importers, exporters and manufacturers with MOOWR, SCOMET classification, EPCG, BIS certification and business registrations, so that they understand the rules that apply to their goods and meet them on time.

    He follows notifications from BIS, CBIC, DGFT, the Income Tax Department, MCA, RBI and SEBI, and explains them in plain language on this website as they are issued.

    He also takes general legal matters, from agreements and notices to civil and consumer cases, and works with a commitment to clarity, accuracy and timely compliance.
